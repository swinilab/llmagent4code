## Scope notes

- **Counter scope.** `db_product_reads_total` and `db_product_read_attempts_total`
  are deliberately Product-scoped narrow probes for ASR-P1 and ASR-A2. All other
  counters are system-wide: `timeouts_total`, `retry_attempts_total`, and
  `transaction_rollbacks_total` fire wherever their mechanism executes, and
  `requests_accepted_total` / `requests_rejected_total` count admission decisions
  across every business endpoint.
- **Cross-cutting scope.** Admission control (ASR-P2), the database time limit
  (ASR-A1), the retry policy (ASR-A2), fail-fast degradation (ASR-A3), and the
  transaction boundary (ASR-A4) are each implemented once and applied
  system-wide. None is reachable only from the endpoint its scenario names.
- **The one scoped tactic.** ASR-P1's maintained copy is applied to Product only.
  Order, Payment, Invoice, and Customer are deliberately excluded because their
  state advances through the workflow and a stale copy would make transaction
  rollbacks externally observable as incorrect. See ADR-1 for the full reasoning.
